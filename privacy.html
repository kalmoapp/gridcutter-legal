Privacy Policy — Grid Cutter

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Grid Cutter ("the app") is developed and maintained by the Grid Cutter team. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

Photos and media

Grid Cutter needs access to your device's photos to let you pick an image and cut it into a grid. All image processing happens entirely on your device — your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server we control. The cut pieces are saved directly back to your device's gallery.

Purchases

Grid Cutter offers an optional PRO unlock (monthly subscription or one-time lifetime purchase). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and verified using RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat receives purchase/transaction information (such as your anonymous user ID and purchase receipt) to confirm your entitlement — it does not receive your photos or any content you create in the app. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy for details on how they handle this data.

What we don't do

We do not require an account or login. We do not collect your name, email, or contacts. We do not run ads or third-party ad trackers. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Children's privacy

Grid Cutter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
